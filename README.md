## NSE IPO Price Band

The **NSE IPO price band** has been fixed at **₹1,700 to ₹1,785 per equity share**.

At the upper end of the price band, the issue is expected to raise around ₹22,562 crore and value NSE at approximately ₹4.42 lakh crore.

The final price investors pay will depend on the IPO's book-building process. Investors should consider the price band together with NSE's earnings, market position and valuation.

## NSE IPO Size

The **NSE IPO size** is approximately **₹22,562 crore** at the upper price band.

The final offer consists of up to around **12.64 crore equity shares** being sold by existing shareholders. Since it is an OFS, the money raised will go to the selling shareholders rather than being used by NSE for expansion or new projects.

## NSE Share Price

The keyword [**NSE Share Price**](https://www.finowings.com/Finance/nse-share-bonus-dividend) is also attracting considerable search interest.

Investors should distinguish between an IPO price and a listed share price. Before NSE's shares begin trading publicly, there is no regular NSE listed-market share price in the same sense as an already-listed company.

The IPO price band of ₹1,700–₹1,785 represents the price at which investors can bid during the public issue. Once the shares are listed, the market will determine the NSE share price based on demand and supply.

## NSE IPO GMP Today

Another highly searched term is [**NSE IPO GMP Today**](https://www.finowings.com/IPO/live-ipo-gmp.php).

GMP, or Grey Market Premium, refers to an unofficial premium at which IPO shares may trade in the grey market before official listing. GMP is not an official exchange price and can change frequently.

Investors searching for **NSE IPO GMP today** should treat grey-market figures as an indication of market sentiment rather than a guaranteed NSE IPO listing price.

An **NSE IPO GMP review** should also consider valuation, subscription demand, fundamentals and broader market conditions.

## NSE IPO Lot Size and Minimum Investment

The **NSE IPO lot size** is **8 shares**.

At the upper price band of ₹1,785:

**8 × ₹1,785 = ₹14,280**

Investors can bid in multiples of the lot size. Current reports also indicate that the retail category has been allocated a minimum of 35% of the issue, while up to 50% can be reserved for qualified institutional buyers and at least 15% for non-institutional investors.
